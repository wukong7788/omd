# Migrating consumers to OMD v0.1.0

This guide describes the public-contract changes in OMD `v0.1.0` and the
boundaries consumer repositories must preserve while migrating. Consumers
should pin `ohmydata==0.1.0` only after the PyPI artifact is published and its
installed version and imports have been verified.

The canonical architecture and acceptance owner is
[`plans/v0.1.0-pit-market-facts.md`](plans/v0.1.0-pit-market-facts.md).

## What v0.1.0 adds

- Typed Tushare requests for stock `daily`, `adj_factor`, and `dividend`.
- Hardened `index_weight`, `daily_basic`, `fund_nav`, and `fund_share` request
  and response scope.
- Immutable snapshot observation receipts and exact response-payload
  `fact_version` identities.
- `AvailabilityEvidence` with explicit source, provider-observed, and snapshot
  timestamps.
- `RawFactEnvelope`, which keeps a canonical row hash separate from the
  response-level fact version and classifies revisions only against an
  explicitly supplied prior row.

Existing endpoint fetches still return `TushareFetchResult` with a defensive
Pandas frame. Polars remains an explicit representation adapter through the
`ohmydata[polars]` extra; endpoint results are not automatically converted or
normalized.

## Fail-closed behavior changes

Consumers should expect earlier, explicit failures in cases that could
previously look complete:

- impossible calendar dates, empty selectors, mixed selector orientations,
  and rows outside the requested symbol/date scope are rejected;
- exact provider-cap responses are rejected where the official endpoint cap
  makes completeness ambiguous;
- incomplete or duplicate pageable adjustment data raises `PaginationError`;
- missing required identity/date fields raise `SchemaMismatchError`;
- current responses with old economic dates remain `PIT_UNPROVEN` unless
  historical availability is backed by valid evidence.

OMD preserves provider-native nulls, zeroes, NaN, infinities, units, duplicate
revision rows, and distinct date roles. Migration code must not turn these
states into zero, deduplicate them, rescale them, or select a revision unless a
named consumer policy owns that transform.

## funmoney_backtest boundary

The funmoney migration should proceed one dataset family at a time and retain a
golden parity artifact before deleting a legacy path. OMD may replace transport
and integrity plumbing, but funmoney continues to own:

- canonical sessions, the operational cutoff, and `first_usable_session`;
- normalized Polars schemas, Parquet/DuckDB paths, dataset commits, and
  publication state;
- universe selection, ETF/QDII eligibility, mapping overrides, feature
  formulas, premium calculations, strategy logic, and execution;
- the next-trading-day policy currently applied to date-only `fund_nav` and
  `fund_share` evidence.

For `fund_nav`, keep `ann_date` and `nav_date` distinct. A date-only
announcement is not an intraday timestamp. For `fund_share`, `fd_share` remains
in Tushare's provider-native ten-thousand-share unit; OMD does not convert it to
market value. For weighted dividend recipes, any minimum accepted coverage
threshold remains a consumer decision.

Do not switch a production provider or synchronize production data based only
on offline tests. The consumer's shadow/parity and deployment authorization
remain separate gates.

## stock_notify boundary

stock_notify may use the typed transports and reusable provider-semantic
recipes, but continues to own its universe, schedules, storage, UI,
notifications, selection thresholds, and publication. It must pin an immutable
published OMD version and preserve golden parity when replacing project-local
provider behavior.

## Upgrade checklist

1. Inventory the exact endpoint calls and fields on the migrated path.
2. Pin an immutable OMD release; do not pin a moving branch.
3. Inject an already initialized official-compatible provider client.
4. Select `EmptyPolicy` and ambiguous coverage policies explicitly.
5. Preserve provider-native dates, units, missingness, ordering, and adjustment
   policy in a golden parity comparison.
6. Store snapshot/observation identities when an exact-version claim is needed.
7. Treat date-only or provider-first-observed availability as `PIT_UNPROVEN`;
   derive sessions and cutoffs in the consumer.
8. Keep the legacy path as rollback until offline parity and any separately
   authorized production shadow both pass.
9. Inspect the installed wheel version and imports before removing legacy code.
