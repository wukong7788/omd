# v0.2.0 — yfinance Market-Data, Fundamentals, and Coverage Contract

Status: `PROPOSED`

## 1. Objective

Add a typed, provider-native yfinance daily-bar and fundamentals integration to
OMD, locked against `yfinance==1.5.1`. Integrate the release first into
`/Users/ron/Documents/stock_notify` to replace its legacy yfinance daily-bar,
FX rate, and fundamentals crawlers, while activating its upgraded Tushare A-share
ETF ingestion. Maintain the offline shadow path for `funmoney_backtest`.

The release must make partial batch failures, invalid adjusted prices, repair
attempts, ticker naming syntax (`^VIX`, `BRK-B`, `0700.HK`, `GBPUSD=X`),
quarterly fundamentals, analyst estimates, valuation ratios, and date coverage
explicit. It must not move a consumer's universe, exchange calendar, strategy
lookback, normalized storage schema, signal generation, or live scheduling into
OMD.

## 2. Evidence and Migration Baseline

The implementation targets two concrete consumers with active production
requirements:

### 2.1 Primary Consumer: `stock_notify`

`stock_notify` (`data/update.py`, `data/build_aetf_prices.py`) currently relies
on `yfinance==1.5.1` across three distinct surfaces:

1. **Daily Bars (`--daily`)**:
   - Downloads 10-symbol batches with `yf.download(auto_adjust=False, threads=False)`;
   - Enforces a strict non-null `Adj Close` policy: missing `Adj Close` is
     treated as a failure and never silently substituted with `Close`;
   - Suffers from Yahoo batch drops where a missing symbol (`KeyError`) or
     partial NaN in a batch aborts the entire price update with `RuntimeError`
     because per-symbol recovery is absent;
   - Requires robust handling of ticker variants including indices (`^VIX`),
     hyphenated share classes (`BRK-B`), and Hong Kong equities (`0700.HK`).

2. **Exchange Rates for Currency Normalization (`_fetch_last_close`)**:
   - Downloads recent bars for currency pairs (`GBPUSD=X`, `{ccy}USD=X`,
     `USD{ccy}=X`) to convert non-USD company market capitalization into
     `market_cap_usd` for unified Screener ranking.

3. **Fundamentals & Valuation Ratios (`--fundamentals`)**:
   - Queries `yf.Ticker` for quarterly financial statements (income statement,
     balance sheet, cash flow statement) to compute latest and prior-year
     comparables (YoY);
   - Extracts valuation multiples (`trailingPE`, `forwardPE`, `pegRatio`,
     `priceToSalesTrailing12Months`, `marketCap`, `enterpriseValue`,
     `sharesOutstanding`);
   - Extracts analyst estimates across 4 horizons (`get_revenue_estimate`,
     `get_earnings_estimate`: current quarter, next quarter, current year, next
     year);
   - Sanitizes timestamps by rejecting epoch-0 (`1970-01-01`) report dates and
     qualifies `quoteType` to filter out funds/ETFs/indices.

4. **A-share ETF Refresh Activation**:
   - `stock_notify` currently pins `ohmydata[tushare]==0.0.4`. Updating
     `stock_notify` to OMD v0.2.0 simultaneously upgrades its Tushare
     dependency, unpausing the suspended A-share ETF price and vintage plane
     pipeline in `data/build_aetf_prices.py`.

### 2.2 Secondary Consumer: `funmoney_backtest`

`funmoney_backtest/data_provider/yfinance.py` downloads multi-symbol daily bars
for systematic ETF strategies:

- requests `auto_adjust=False`, extracts provider-native OHLC, `Adj Close`, and
  `Volume`, and validates that adjusted values are absent;
- requires session coverage over caller-derived XNYS exchange calendars;
- runs as an offline shadow before any production cutover.

Before implementation, freeze characterization tests for `yfinance==1.5.1` and
the existing consumer adapters. No real Yahoo response may be committed;
fixtures must be synthetic and structurally representative.

## 3. Ownership Boundary

### OMD owns

- typed yfinance requests and results for daily bars, FX exchange rate tickers,
  and quarterly fundamentals / valuation / estimates;
- acceptance of valid Yahoo symbol syntax (`^VIX`, `BRK-B`, `0700.HK`,
  `GBPUSD=X`);
- dependency loading and locked version check against `yfinance==1.5.1`;
- provider call parameters, multi-index flattening, and response-shape
  normalization;
- explicit adjustment, empty-result, batch-completeness, and repair policies;
- request-scope, schema, key, numeric, ordering, and coverage validation;
- fundamentals data validation, `quoteType` qualification, and epoch-0 date
  sanitization;
- classified retry behavior with injected clock/sleep;
- per-attempt provenance and repair receipts;
- provider-native immutable snapshot/replay integration;
- a coverage validator that checks caller-supplied expected sessions.

### Consumers own

- symbol-universe selection and configuration files;
- exchange selection and construction of canonical sessions;
- required lookback length and signal/trade dates;
- authoritative listing dates or other security-master facts;
- currency multiplier derivation and screener valuation calculations (e.g.
  `market_cap_usd = market_cap * fx_rate`);
- whether an unverified leading history prefix is acceptable;
- adjusted-bar and fundamentals normalization into a consumer schema (e.g.,
  Parquet store, DuckDB);
- amount/turnover derivation, features, backtests, signals, brokers, storage,
  notifications, and schedules.

OMD must not import `exchange_calendars`, infer XNYS from ticker spelling, or
hard-code the R10 A0 200-session requirement.

## 4. Listing-Date Boundary

`history_metadata.firstTradeDate` is optional Yahoo metadata and is not an
authoritative exchange listing date. v0.2.0 may preserve it as a provider-native
metadata observation with provenance, but must not name it `listing_date`, use
it to claim complete history, or silently exempt earlier expected sessions.

Because v0.2.0 has no authoritative listing-date source, coverage policies are:

- `STRICT_EXPECTED_SESSIONS`: every caller-supplied expected session must have
  one valid bar for every required symbol;
- `FROM_FIRST_VALID_BAR`: leading expected sessions before the first valid bar
  are reported as `unverified_prefix_sessions` rather than treated as missing;
  this is an explicit caller opt-in and never claims that the symbol was not
  listed;
- `EXPLICIT_COVERAGE_START`: the caller supplies a coverage start per symbol
  from its own security master. OMD checks sessions from that date onward but
  does not judge the authority of the supplied date.

The funmoney R10 migration must use `STRICT_EXPECTED_SESSIONS` for its existing
ETF universe. It must not weaken the production gate to
`FROM_FIRST_VALID_BAR` merely to accommodate missing data.

Add an optional extra locked strictly to the tested `1.5.1` baseline required by
the primary consumers:

```toml
[project.optional-dependencies]
yfinance = ["pandas>=2.0,<3.0", "yfinance==1.5.1"]
```

The version is locked against `1.5.1` to match `stock_notify` and

## 7. Public Contract

The public slice exposes names equivalent to:

```python
YFinanceDailyBarsRequest
YFinanceDailyBarsResult
YFinanceFundamentalsRequest
YFinanceFundamentalsResult
YFinanceValuationSnapshot
YFinanceQuarterlyFinancials
YFinanceAnalystEstimates
YFinanceClient
YFinanceAdjustmentMode
YFinanceBatchPolicy
YFinanceRepairPolicy
YFinanceCoveragePolicy
YFinanceCoverageRequest
YFinanceCoverageReport
validate_yfinance_daily_bar_coverage
```

Final names are frozen in the implementation guide, but the following
semantics are mandatory.

### 7.1 Daily Bars Request & Supported Symbols

`YFinanceDailyBarsRequest` contains:

- a non-empty, ordered, duplicate-free tuple of symbols;
- explicit `start_date`;
- an explicitly named exclusive end boundary (`end_date_exclusive`), or a
  documented conversion from an inclusive public boundary to yfinance's
  provider-native boundary;
- daily interval only for the v0.2.0 slice;
- explicit adjustment mode;
- explicit empty/batch policy;
- explicit repair policy;
- optional timeout and bounded retry policy from OMD core.

#### Symbol Syntax Support

Symbol validation must accept provider-native Yahoo symbol conventions required
by active consumers:

- standard US equities: `AAPL`, `MSFT`;
- hyphenated / dotted share classes: `BRK-B` (or mapped from `BRK.B`);
- major index tickers: `^VIX`, `^GSPC`, `^NDX`;
- regional exchange equities: `0700.HK`, `9988.HK`;
- currency and foreign exchange pairs: `GBPUSD=X`, `EURUSD=X`, `USDCNY=X`.

The request must not read environment variables, YAML universes, consumer
paths, or `.env`. Symbols and dates are supplied by the caller.

### 7.2 Adjustment mode

Replace the ambiguous public boolean with an enum:

- `RAW_WITH_ADJ_CLOSE`: request raw OHLC plus Yahoo `Adj Close` and preserve
  both independently;
- `AUTO_ADJUSTED`: request Yahoo-adjusted OHLC and mark that no independent raw
  versus adjusted comparison is available.

No mode may synthesize a missing `Adj Close` from `Close`. Any derived factor
must be an explicit recipe with its own validation and formula identifier.

The `stock_notify` and `funmoney` parity paths use `RAW_WITH_ADJ_CLOSE`.

### 7.3 Daily Bars Result

`YFinanceDailyBarsResult` provides a defensive copy of the provider-native
Pandas frame plus:

- exact ordered requested and returned symbols;
- effective request parameters;
- yfinance version (`1.5.1`);
- attempt provenance;
- batch and per-symbol outcome records;
- adjustment-field presence and invalid-value counts;
- repair receipt when recovery was attempted;
- stable ordering by symbol and provider session date;
- clean standard DataFrame conversion (`to_pandas()`) containing normalized
  columns: `symbol, date, open, high, low, close, adj_close, volume`.

It must preserve nulls and provider-native values.

### 7.4 Fundamentals Request & Contract

`YFinanceFundamentalsRequest` queries symbol fundamentals via `yf.Ticker`
with caller-specified scope:

- ordered non-empty tuple of symbols;
- optional flags to include statements, valuation ratios, and analyst estimates;
- classified retry and timeout policy.

`YFinanceFundamentalsResult` returns structured, typed data per symbol:

1. **Valuation & Multiples (`YFinanceValuationSnapshot`)**:
   - `trailing_pe` (`trailingPE`);
   - `forward_pe` (`forwardPE`);
   - `peg_ratio` (`pegRatio`);
   - `price_to_sales` (`priceToSalesTrailing12Months`);
   - `forward_eps` (`forwardEps`);
   - `market_cap` (`marketCap` / `fast_info.market_cap`);
   - `enterprise_value` (`enterpriseValue`);
   - `shares_outstanding` (`sharesOutstanding` / `fast_info.shares`);
   - `currency` (`currency` / `financialCurrency`).

2. **Quarterly Financial Statements (`YFinanceQuarterlyFinancials`)**:
   Extracts both the latest quarterly report value and the prior year same quarter
   value (YoY comparable):
   - **Income Statement**: `total_revenue`, `gross_profit`, `operating_income`,
     `ebitda`, `diluted_eps`, `basic_eps`, `normalized_eps`, `net_income`;
   - **Cash Flow Statement**: `operating_cash_flow`, `free_cash_flow`,
     `capital_expenditure`;
   - **Balance Sheet**: `total_debt`, `cash`, `net_debt`, `shareholders_equity`.

3. **Analyst Estimates (`YFinanceAnalystEstimates`)**:
   Extracts four horizons (`current_q`, `next_q`, `current_y`, `next_y`):
   - `revenue_est_{current_q, next_q, current_y, next_y}`;
   - `eps_est_{current_q, next_q, current_y, next_y}`.

4. **Growth Hints & Metadata**:
   - `revenue_growth_hint` (`revenueGrowth`), `eps_growth_hint` (`earningsGrowth`);
   - `report_date` (derived from `mostRecentQuarter` or income statement date);
   - `quote_type` (`quoteType`, e.g. `EQUITY`, `ETF`, `INDEX`, `MUTUALFUND`).

5. **Null Semantics**:
   Missing fields remain `None` / `null`. They must never be coerced to `0.0`
   or plausible synthetic defaults.

## 8. Response Validation

Validation occurs before a result is considered successful.

### 8.1 Structural validation

- require a Pandas DataFrame;
- accept only characterized single-level and multi-index column layouts;
- reject ambiguous or unknown index/column orientations;
- require the fields implied by the selected adjustment mode;
- reject null, malformed, or duplicate `(symbol, session_date)` keys;
- reject returned symbols outside request scope;
- reject dates outside effective request bounds;
- stable-sort output without keep-first/keep-last deduplication;
- preserve an allowed empty result only under an explicit policy.

### 8.2 Numeric validation

- price fields present on a usable row must be real, finite, and strictly
  positive;
- volume must be real, finite, and non-negative when present;
- reject `high < low` and OHLC range contradictions;
- never convert missing, NaN, infinity, zero, or negative adjusted prices into
  plausible values;
- do not derive dollar amount inside the provider-native result.

### 8.3 Batch completeness

A non-empty batch is not success by itself. Every requested symbol receives an
explicit outcome:

- `COMPLETE`;
- `EMPTY`;
- `INVALID_SCHEMA`;
- `INVALID_VALUES`;
- `MISSING_EXPECTED_SESSIONS`;
- `RECOVERED`;
- `RECOVERY_FAILED`.

Under strict batch policy, any non-success outcome fails the whole request and
preserves the per-symbol diagnostics without embedding raw rows in the error.

### 8.4 Fundamentals validation

- `report_date` must reject epoch-0 or placeholder dates (e.g., `1970-01-01`
  or values before `1990-01-01`);
- numeric valuation ratios, market caps, and statement metrics must be finite
  when present;
- symbols with non-equity `quoteType` (e.g., `ETF`, `INDEX`, `MUTUALFUND`,
  `ETN`) are explicitly flagged with `is_excluded=True` and reason recorded;
- symbols where all core fundamentals fields are absent return typed `EMPTY`
  outcome rather than silent partial success.

Schema/numeric failures raise `SchemaMismatchError`. Missing required symbols
or sessions raise `CoverageError`.

## 9. Coverage Validation

`validate_yfinance_daily_bar_coverage` accepts:

- a validated daily-bar result or frame;
- ordered required symbols;
- exact expected sessions supplied by the caller, globally or per symbol;
- one explicit coverage policy;
- optional explicit coverage starts for
  `EXPLICIT_COVERAGE_START`.

A valid adjusted-price observation is non-null, real, finite, and greater than
zero. Coverage is checked against valid observations, not merely key presence.

The report includes:

- missing symbols;
- missing expected sessions per symbol;
- invalid-value sessions per symbol;
- future-only or out-of-range rows;
- unverified leading-prefix sessions;
- effective coverage start and end;
- selected policy and deterministic status.

The validator never computes exchange sessions and never decides how many
sessions a strategy needs. Consumers may fail on any report field more
strictly than the selected OMD policy.

## 10. Repair and Retry Contract

Retry and repair are distinct:

- retry repeats the same classified transient provider call;
- repair changes the provider operation for a bounded set of failed symbols.

Authentication, unsupported parameters, malformed schema, deterministic
numeric failures, and coverage failures are not transient retries.

If `YFinanceRepairPolicy.PER_SYMBOL` is selected:

1. run and validate the original batch request;
2. identify only failed symbols;
3. fetch each failed symbol through the characterized per-symbol API with the
   explicit yfinance repair option;
4. validate each recovery result under the same request bounds and adjustment
   mode;
5. merge only unique, in-scope keys;
6. re-run whole-result validation and caller-supplied coverage validation;
7. emit a receipt binding the original outcome, repair parameters, attempts,
   returned key counts, and final status.

Repair must never overwrite or conceal the first response in snapshot mode.
If repair changes an already valid key's value, record it as revision evidence
and fail unless a separately accepted conflict policy exists.

No automatic alternate ticker, exchange, interval, adjustment mode, date
range, forward fill, backward fill, or imputation is allowed.

## 11. Provenance, Snapshot, and Revision Semantics

Provenance binds at least:

- provider and endpoint/operation;
- exact effective parameters and ordered symbols;
- adjustment, empty, batch, repair, and coverage policies;
- yfinance and OMD versions;
- attempt timing and classified outcomes;
- response/snapshot hashes without credentials or raw error payloads.

Raw batch and repair responses are separate immutable observations. Replay
validates request identity, serialization version, member hashes, and result
schema before deserialization.

Yahoo historical prices may be revised. Same request with different bytes is
revision evidence, not cache corruption and not permission to overwrite a
frozen snapshot. OMD records both observations; consumers decide which
dataset version becomes canonical.

## 14. Acceptance Gates

v0.2.0 is acceptable only when:

1. every requested symbol has an explicit outcome;
2. strict mode cannot accept a non-empty but partial batch;
3. invalid adjusted prices cannot pass coverage through key presence alone;
4. repair is explicit, bounded, revalidated, and provenance-bound;
5. fundamentals extracts quarterly statements, valuation multiples, and analyst
   estimates while strictly preserving nulls and rejecting epoch-0 dates;
6. symbol syntax for `^VIX`, `BRK-B`, `0700.HK`, and `GBPUSD=X` is supported;
7. `stock_notify` integration succeeds for daily bars, FX, fundamentals, and
   unpaused Tushare A-share ETF ingestion;
8. no `firstTradeDate` value is presented as authoritative listing evidence;
9. OMD does not select calendars or strategy lookbacks;
10. raw and repaired observations remain immutable and distinguishable;
11. historical Yahoo revisions are preserved as revisions rather than
    overwritten;
12. funmoney offline shadow proves request, data, quality, and signal parity;
13. all OMD release checks pass on Python 3.11 and 3.12;
14. public artifacts contain no real responses, credentials, consumer paths,
    or private configuration;
15. production remains on the legacy provider until separately authorized.

## 15. Explicit Non-goals

- authoritative security master or listing-date service;
- exchange-calendar selection or calendar distribution;
- automatic ticker remapping or delisting inference;
- intraday bars, options, news, or streaming quotes;
- universal cross-provider normalized bar schema;
- consumer Parquet layout, incremental merge, or canonical publication;
- strategy data requirements, backtesting, signal generation, or execution;
- automatic PyPI publication or consumer/live cutover.

## 16. Rollback and Failure Handling

- OMD introduction is additive; consumers' legacy providers remain available
  throughout validation.
- Any request, adjustment, date, timezone, null, or signal-parity drift blocks
  cutover and leaves production unchanged.
- A failed repair returns a typed failure with receipt; callers must not publish
  the partial result.
- A yfinance version differing from `1.5.1` blocks the provider until a new
  characterization and OMD release are reviewed.
- A Yahoo historical revision creates a new immutable observation and requires
  consumer canonical-replay review; it never silently rewrites accepted
  research evidence.
