# v0.1.3 — ETF Benchmark & Constituent Vintage Plane

Status: **READY_FOR_EXECUTION**

Prepared: 2026-08-09

Execution model: **Sol–Luna review-first**. Sol owns this contract, scope,
preflight, independent evidence review, and final acceptance. Luna may implement
only a frozen brief derived from this document. Any contract edit invalidates the

## 1. Decision and objective

The next OMD slice is a data-engineering and forward-evidence project, not an
economic experiment.

Build the smallest append-only source-fact plane that can repeatedly capture and
version:

1. observed ETF-to-benchmark-index mappings;
2. observed exact-date index constituent weights;
3. the availability, observation, snapshot, payload, and revision evidence needed
   for a consumer to resolve those facts fail-closed at its own decision cutoff.

The visible outcome is a deterministic, replayable producer/registry bundle from
which consumers can distinguish verified forward observations from facts that are
current-only, not yet observed, incomplete, or historically unproven.

Success means that future Dynamic Universe and look-through research can accumulate
honest source vintages. It does **not** mean that historical PIT coverage exists or
that any ETF, exposure, universe change, or strategy promotion is justified.

## 2. Why this slice, and why now

The motivating consumer, `funmoney_backtest`, has completed Iter5.5 without a
promotion. Its remaining historical full-market Dynamic Universe and ETF
look-through questions share one data bottleneck: current ETF identity, current
benchmark mappings, and current index weights cannot be silently backfilled as
historical PIT facts.

This slice has higher reusable value than a QDII-NAV-only extension because it
unblocks the source layer for both candidate identity/clone analysis and constituent
look-through. It must not read returns, candidate performance, recent price moves,
or any Iter5.5 economic label.

## 3. Current-state review

OMD `0.1.2` already provides the foundations and they must be reused rather than
reimplemented:

- `SnapshotStore` append-only observations, replay, integrity validation, and
  `provider_first_observed_at`;
- `AvailabilityEvidence` and `RawFactEnvelope`;
- `capture_tushare_result(...)` with deterministic provider-native frame
  serialization;
- typed `EtfBasicRequest` and `IndexWeightRequest`;
- `build_etf_index_mapping_observations(...)` with current-only, unchanged,
  revised, and missing mapping statuses;
- `audit_index_weight_vintage(...)` with explicit percent units, weight integrity,
  expected-count evidence, and unproven retrieval/economic completeness;
- `build_lookthrough_source_bundle(...)` for immutable manifest-only evidence.

The actual gap is operational and contractual:

- no first-class producer coordinates repeated bounded fetch/capture/audit runs;
- no durable registry projects many immutable observations into a stable queryable
  vintage ledger;
- mapping and weight revision transitions are not exposed together in one
  cross-retrieval manifest;
- no provider-neutral source-fact manifest contract lets consumers fail closed
  without importing Tushare-specific frame assumptions;
- no machine-readable coverage/gap report separates forward-observed evidence from
  historically unproven facts across a declared ETF/index inventory.

Historical `provider_first_observed_at`, old provider payloads, benchmark revision
vintages, or index-weight vintages that were never captured remain unavailable.
This is an evidence gap, not permission to infer them.

## 4. Ownership boundary

### 4.1 OMD owns

- typed provider requests and provider-specific field/unit/pagination semantics;
- deterministic fetch-result capture into immutable snapshots;
- source event/effective dates only when explicitly supplied by the source;
- retrieval time, provider-first-observed time, snapshot and payload identities;
- append-only observation and revision lineage;
- source-fact quality, completeness, unsupported, missing, and PIT-unproven states;
- provider-neutral registry records that preserve provider-specific lineage;
- an idempotent producer API that receives credentials/client and storage from its
  caller.

### 4.2 Consumers own

- canonical calendars, trading cutoffs, and `first_usable_session`;
- `consumer_dataset_committed_at` and publication schedules;
- ETF universe, candidate eligibility, clone-group and carrier selection;
- style, cluster, exposure, overlap, novelty, scores, ranks, and labels;
- normalized consumer datasets and artifact locations;
- strategy, backtest, signal, live execution, and promotion decisions.

OMD must never emit a strategy-ready universe or silently convert observation time
into a consumer trading session.

## 5. Frozen source semantics

Every registry record must preserve these concepts separately when applicable:

```text
source_event_date
source_effective_from
source_effective_to
source_available_at
availability_basis
availability_precision
provider_first_observed_at
snapshot_fetched_at
snapshot_identity
observation_identity
request_identity
payload_sha256
fact_version
revision_status
supersedes_fact_version
quality_flags
```

Rules:

1. `source_available_at` is populated only when a provider or official source
   supplies auditable publication evidence. It must not be derived from a business
   date, an effective date, or retrieval time.
2. `provider_first_observed_at` is when this OMD snapshot store first observed the
   exact payload/fact version. It cannot be backdated.
3. `snapshot_fetched_at` records the exact retrieval observation used by the
   snapshot. It is not automatically a legal consumer session.
4. `source_effective_*` may describe provider-native business validity, but does not
   prove availability.
5. `supersedes_fact_version` is present only when exact same-key prior evidence
   supports the transition. No latest-value overwrite is permitted.
6. Missing values stay missing. No current mapping, constituent, weight, or
   classification may be used as a historical fallback.

## 6. Public contract slices

Exact class and module names may be adjusted during Sol preflight to match existing
package conventions, but the semantics and ownership boundary are frozen.

### 6.1 Provider-neutral vintage records

Introduce narrow immutable records under `ohmydata.core` or another reviewed shared
module:

```python
SourceFactObservation
SourceFactRevisionRef
SourceFactRegistryManifest
```

The core types may contain provider ID, endpoint/source type, opaque source key,
time evidence, identities, status, and quality flags. They must not contain Tushare
column names, ETF selection logic, Pandas/Polars objects, canonical sessions, or
consumer classifications.

Required registry behavior:

- append only;
- atomic manifest publication;
- exact payload/snapshot lineage;
- deterministic ordering and manifest identity;
- idempotent repeated registration of an identical observation;
- distinct preservation of changed payloads;
- fail-closed rejection of conflicting identities, forged lineage, backdated first
  observation, and in-place overwrite.

### 6.2 ETF benchmark mapping projection

Project captured `etf_basic` observations into one versioned row per observed ETF
mapping fact. Minimum provider projection:

```text
etf_code
benchmark_index_code
list_status
mapping_source_type
source_effective_from
source_effective_to
source_available_at
availability_basis
availability_precision
provider_first_observed_at
snapshot_fetched_at
snapshot_identity
observation_identity
request_identity
payload_sha256
fact_version
revision_status
supersedes_fact_version
quality_flags
```

`mapping_source_type` may be `PROVIDER_DECLARED`, `OFFICIAL_DOCUMENT`, or
`UNKNOWN`, but this slice needs to implement only source types backed by a concrete
provider requirement. A consumer manual override must never be serialized as a
provider fact.

If `etf_basic` supplies only a currently observed `index_code`, emit current-only,
historically-unproven evidence. Do not invent mapping effective intervals.

### 6.3 Index constituent-weight projection

Project captured `index_weight` observations into one immutable version per
`(index_code, provider trade_date, observation/fact version)` and retain constituent
rows beneath that version.

Minimum version metadata:

```text
index_code
provider_trade_date
source_available_at
availability_basis
availability_precision
provider_first_observed_at
snapshot_fetched_at
snapshot_identity
observation_identity
request_identity
payload_sha256
fact_version
revision_status
supersedes_fact_version
native_weight_unit
conversion_identity
observed_component_count
expected_component_count
expected_count_basis
coverage_weight
retrieval_status
economic_completeness_status
quality_flags
```

Constituent rows preserve provider-native weight and the named percent-to-decimal
conversion. Duplicate constituents, null/non-finite/out-of-range weights, partial
pagination, abnormal totals, and unknown completeness remain explicit failures or
flags. A weight sum near 100% is never sufficient evidence of complete membership.

### 6.4 Producer

Add an explicit producer/orchestrator that:

1. accepts a caller-constructed typed provider client, `SnapshotStore`, registry
   destination, explicit aware `observed_at`, and a declared ETF/index scope;
2. fetches only reviewed bounded requests;
3. captures each validated result before projection;
4. validates/audits the mapping and weight observations;
5. appends registry records and emits a deterministic run manifest;
6. reports every requested item as captured, missing, unsupported, partial, or
   failed; and
7. performs no hidden retry, scheduling, credential lookup, environment access,
   universe discovery, or consumer publication.

A no-network assembly/replay mode must allow deterministic tests and rebuilding a
manifest from already captured observations. Live provider calls remain separately
authorized integration activity.

### 6.5 Resolution manifest, not consumer as-of policy

OMD may expose source-level resolution for a supplied cutoff timestamp:

```text
VERIFIED_SOURCE_OBSERVATION
UNKNOWN_FAIL_CLOSED
NOT_YET_OBSERVED
CURRENT_ONLY_HISTORICAL_UNPROVEN
INCOMPLETE_SOURCE_OBSERVATION
```

Such resolution may use only source/observation timestamps and exact fact versions.
It must not emit `first_usable_session` or map to a trading calendar. A consumer may
later combine:

```text
source/provider availability evidence
provider_first_observed_at
consumer_dataset_committed_at
consumer canonical cutoff policy
```

to derive its own legal session.

## 7. Minimal artifact contract

One producer run writes a new immutable bundle, never overwriting an existing one:

```text
pit_etf_benchmark_constituent_bundle/
├── etf_benchmark_vintages.parquet
├── index_weight_vintages.parquet
├── index_weight_constituents.parquet
├── source_resolution_manifest.parquet
├── coverage_and_gap_report.json
└── machine_gate.json
```

Parquet is a publication representation, not the source of truth for snapshot
lineage. Every row must bind back to immutable OMD snapshot identities and hashes.
The implementation may use an existing manifest format if Sol proves it covers the
same fields and gates; do not create a duplicate storage system merely to match the
suggested filenames.

Real downloaded/provider data, local snapshot stores, and generated consumer
artifacts must remain ignored and must never be committed.

## 8. Machine gates

`machine_gate.json` passes only when all applicable checks pass:

1. **APPEND_ONLY** — an old observation or registry version was not overwritten or
   silently changed.
2. **NO_FUTURE_VISIBILITY** — cutoff resolution never selects an observation whose
   applicable availability/observation anchor is after the supplied cutoff.
3. **EXACT_LINEAGE** — every resolved fact traces to request, snapshot,
   observation, fact version, and payload hash.
4. **UNKNOWN_FAILS_CLOSED** — absent mapping, availability, weight vintage, or
   completeness cannot become a verified row through fallback.
5. **REVISION_PRESERVED** — changed same-key facts append a new version and retain
   the prior version and explicit transition evidence.
6. **WEIGHT_INTEGRITY** — duplicate, invalid, partial, or abnormal constituent data
   is rejected or explicitly non-ready.
7. **DETERMINISTIC_REPLAY** — the same registry and cutoff produce the same ordered
   output and bundle identity.
8. **NO_ECONOMIC_LABEL_DEPENDENCY** — no API, input, schema, or fixture requires
   returns, performance, score, rank, target, action, or promotion fields.
9. **PUBLIC_SAFETY** — no token, credential, real provider payload, consumer
   account data, or private artifact is tracked.

The consumer's B6-B1-2 canonical no-drift matrix belongs to the later funmoney
consumer integration gate, not OMD unit acceptance. This OMD change must not edit
the consumer repository.

## 12. Acceptance and stopping rules

### Engineering acceptance

Sol may accept the first stage only when:

- every declared ETF/index request has an explicit captured/missing/unsupported/
  partial/failure status;
- no current fact is silently resolved into an earlier cutoff;
- repeated runs preserve immutable vintages and deterministic identities;
- at least one synthetic revision proves old and new versions remain replayable;
- a later real forward observation may be appended without schema or identity
  ambiguity;
- public APIs and migration docs state the consumer ownership boundary;
- all required checks pass and the diff contains no unrelated or sensitive data.

A real later vintage is not required to accept the code contract, but is required
before claiming the forward producer has been operationally proven against a real
revision/transition.

### Stop and fail-closed conditions

Stop historical reconstruction and return an explicit blocked/unknown status if:

- the provider cannot supply historical availability or old payloads;
- only a current mapping or current weight snapshot is obtainable;
- success would require inferring history from an ETF name, current webpage,
  present-day index relationship, or consumer manual mapping;
- coverage can improve only by weakening completeness or provenance gates;
- implementation needs returns or economic labels to choose the fetch scope;
- a provider-specific assumption would leak into the provider-neutral core;
- a data-contract change would require editing consumer strategy/live behavior.

Failure constrains only the data layer:

```text
historical PIT reconstructability unavailable or incomplete
```

It does not imply that Dynamic Universe or look-through mechanisms are invalid,
that the current ETF universe is complete, or that any ETF is incompatible.
