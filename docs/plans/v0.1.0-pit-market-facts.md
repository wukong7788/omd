# OMD v0.1.0 — PIT-Ready Constituent Market Facts

Status: **PLANNED — CONTRACT REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION**

This document owns the scope, contracts, sequencing, and acceptance gates for
OMD `v0.1.0`. The repository-level [`PLAN.md`](../../PLAN.md) remains the
canonical roadmap; this plan is the detailed contract for the `v0.1.0`
vertical slice.

## 1. Release Objective

`v0.1.0` delivers versioned, snapshot-backed, availability-aware constituent
market facts for point-in-time research. It adds typed Tushare stock daily,
adjustment-factor, and stock-dividend endpoints, hardens the semantics of the
existing index-weight and related market-fact endpoints, and separates source
publication time from the time OMD first observed an exact provider payload.

It does not infer consumer trading sessions, construct strategy features, own
consumer datasets, or claim historical vintage availability that cannot be
proven.

The smallest end-to-end data chain is:

```text
index_weight
    -> stock daily
    -> stock adj_factor
    -> daily_basic
    -> stock dividend
    -> snapshot/version/availability evidence
```

This chain is intended to support later consumer-owned research into
constituent returns, breadth, return-contribution concentration, and PIT
dividend inputs without embedding those feature definitions in OMD.

## 2. Dependency Decision

Development of the `v0.1.0` contracts and endpoints does **not** require every
unchecked item in the repository roadmap to be completed first.

The following work may proceed in parallel:

- OMD endpoint-semantic contract review and synthetic fixture design;
- raw-fact, snapshot-version, and availability metadata design;

- the remaining funmoney consumer parity and canonical-provider cutover work.

- the funmoney canonical-provider cutover must complete its critical-path
  inventory and baseline/data-snapshot freeze before that cutover is merged;
- no production synchronization is authorized by an offline cutover or by this
  plan; deployment is a separate consumer gate;
- unchecked Phase 3c parity work must be accepted before claiming the funmoney
  Polars migration complete;
- the `v0.1.0` release may not be declared complete until all P0 acceptance
  gates in this document and the repository global acceptance gates pass.

Existing unchecked migration items therefore do not block starting `v0.1.0`,
but they remain real release, migration, or production-readiness obligations
where explicitly referenced.

## 4. Time, Availability, and Revision Contract

### 4.1 Required time layers

OMD facts and snapshot metadata distinguish:

- `source_available_at`: source-side publication time, nullable and never
  assigned false precision;
- `provider_first_observed_at`: the first time OMD observed the exact payload
  version, mandatory for snapshot-backed version claims;
- `snapshot_fetched_at`: the execution time of the current fetch, mandatory;
- provider-native event and effective dates, preserved as distinct named
  fields with documented roles.

OMD also records:

- `availability_basis`: `source_declared`, `inferred_schedule`,
  `provider_first_observed`, or `unknown`;
- `availability_precision`: `timestamp`, `date`, `session`, or `unknown`;
- `revision_status`, `fact_version`, and `payload_hash`.

`first_usable_session` is not an OMD raw-fact field. A consumer derives it from
the legal availability anchor, provider observation evidence, its dataset
commit time, canonical calendar, and operational cutoff.

If historical availability or vintage cannot be proven, the contract is:

```text
first_usable_session = null
quality_flags += PIT_UNPROVEN
consumer action = fail closed
```

A value first fetched now must not be represented as the historical provider
value merely because its economic or announcement date is old.

### 4.2 Append-only revision behavior

The same provider primary key may yield multiple payload versions. OMD must:

- retain the old fact version;
- append the newly observed version;
- preserve the first-observed time for each exact version;
- make the lineage and revision status inspectable;
- prevent a replay from silently substituting a later revision.

## 5. Fact Contracts

### 5.1 Raw fact envelope

Raw provider facts use endpoint-specific native fields plus metadata such as:

- provider and endpoint;
- entity identity and provider primary key;
- provider-native fields and all event/effective dates;
- availability and observation metadata;
- snapshot ID, request hash, payload hash, schema version, and adapter version;
- revision status and quality flags.

Raw facts do not require a universal scalar `value`, `method_version`,
`universe_version`, or row-level `coverage`. Coverage is normally a query,
batch, panel, or derived-result property.

### 5.2 Derived fact envelope

Derived facts and recipes use a separate contract containing, as applicable:

- feature name, entity, feature date, value, and unit;
- input snapshot identities;
- method and universe versions;
- weight/effective date;
- count and weight coverage;
- explicit missing-data policy;
- quality flags.

No universal fact table is required for raw endpoint rows and derived recipe
outputs.

## 6. Endpoint Semantic Manifests

Each P0 endpoint must document and test:

- provider primary key and entity identity;
- all provider-native units;
- date fields and their semantic roles;
- null, zero, non-finite, and empty-result semantics;
- stable ordering and duplicate policy;
- page/window behavior and truncation detection;
- mutable fields and append-version revision behavior;
- required fields and schema-drift handling.

Stock-dividend data must preserve its distinct reporting, announcement,
approval, implementation, record, ex-dividend, payment, and process-state
fields when provided. A generic `observation_date` must not hide these roles.

Index weights must distinguish query/snapshot dates from economic effective
dates and must not imply an unproven next-period applicability rule.

## 8. funmoney Migration and Research Sequence

This sequence preserves causal attribution by separating transport migration,
shadow research facts, and strategy changes.

### P0 — Migration correctness only

- **M0a: critical-path inventory and baseline freeze.** Record canonical
  signal/backtest paths, production-sync paths, provider configuration,
  baseline artifacts, data hashes, and rollback instructions.
- **M0b: canonical daily-provider cutover.** Map `tushare` to OMD, retain the
  explicit `tushare_legacy` rollback, and preserve schema, ordering, dates,
  values, adjustment policy, and signals.
- **M0c: deployment gate.** Run authorized read-only production shadow and
  artifact/hash parity separately; production synchronization requires its own
  authorization.
- [x] **M1: full direct-call inventory.** Production, research, display, legacy
  rollback, and archived call sites now have explicit current/target ownership
  in the consumer-owned
  `funmoney_backtest/docs/plans/omd_tushare_direct_call_inventory.md`.
  M2 code execution remains paused while the consumer refactor is active.
- **M2: migrate transport one dataset family at a time.** Recommended order:
  `index_weight`; stock daily plus adjustment factor; `daily_basic`; dividend;
  then `fund_share`/`fund_nav`. Each slice requires its own parity artifact.

The transport phase must not change feature formulas, universe membership,
PIT alignment, or strategy behavior.

### P1 — Shadow research and portfolio audit

- **M3:** produce consumer-owned shadow facts for component breadth, return
  contribution concentration, atomic dividend-carry/yield-change inputs, and
  look-through exposure.
- **M4:** run a post-blend exposure audit that records strategy contribution,
  constituent overlap, industry/style/cluster exposure, and later outcomes.
  It must not cap, project, replace candidates, or change risk budgets.

### P2 — Independent strategy challengers

- **M5.1:** exposure-aware construction;
- **M5.2:** momentum quality;
- **M5.3:** dividend carry/crowding gate;
- **M5.4:** intramonth interrupt.

Each challenger requires an independent baseline, estimand, event table, cost
model, failure scope, and Data/Mechanism/Carrier/Action/Production conclusion.

## 9. Acceptance Evidence

`v0.1.0` acceptance requires more than successful command exits:

- offline Python 3.11 and 3.12 tests;
- Ruff, format, Pyright, build, and `git diff --check` gates;
- snapshot/replay and exact-version inspection;
- endpoint-by-endpoint schema, unit, date-role, missingness, pagination, and
  revision evidence;
- no secret or licensed provider data in source, fixtures, logs, or artifacts;
- consumer parity evidence for any migrated path;
- no claim of production readiness based solely on offline or shadow results.

only from an accepted brief whose endpoint fields, keys, units, date roles,
revision behavior, and failure policies are explicit.
